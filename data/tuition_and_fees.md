# Tuition and Fee Schedule

**School:** Greenfield International School  
**Document type:** Fictional tuition and fee schedule for RAG testing  
**Academic Year:** 2026–2027  
**Last updated:** 1 September 2026  

## Annual Tuition by Grade Level

Tuition covers core academic instruction, textbooks, library access, standard digital learning resources, and regular campus facilities.

- **Middle School (Grades 7–8):** $14,500 per academic year ($7,250 per semester)
- **Junior High (Grades 9–10):** $16,800 per academic year ($8,400 per semester)
- **Senior High (Grades 11–12):** $18,500 per academic year ($9,250 per semester)

Tuition invoices are issued by the Finance Office 30 days before each payment deadline.

## Application and Enrollment Fees

- **Application Fee:** $150 (one-time, non-refundable, due upon submission of admission application)
- **New Student Registration and Enrollment Fee:** $1,000 (one-time fee for newly admitted students to secure enrollment placement)
- **Re-enrollment Deposit for Returning Students:** $500 (annual deposit due by 15 April, credited toward Semester 1 tuition)

## Payment Options and Schedules

Families can choose one of three payment plans:

1. **Full Annual Payment (Single Installment):**
   - Due date: 15 August 2026
   - Benefit: 3% early-payment discount on the annual tuition amount.

2. **Semester Plan (Two Installments):**
   - Semester 1 Payment: Due 15 August 2026 (50% of annual tuition)
   - Semester 2 Payment: Due 15 January 2027 (50% of annual tuition)

3. **Monthly Plan (Ten Equal Installments):**
   - Runs from August through May. Payments are due on the 1st day of each month.
   - An administrative processing fee of $250 per academic year applies to the monthly plan.

Late payments incur a 1.5% late penalty per month on any outstanding balance after a 7-day grace period.

## Additional and Optional Services Fees

- **Technology and Lab Fee:** $450 per academic year (covers high-speed campus Wi-Fi, software licenses, IT helpdesk, and science laboratory supplies).
- **School Uniform Set:** Approximately $220 for the standard starter kit (2 formal uniforms, 2 daily shirts, and 1 physical education kit).
- **School Bus Transportation (Optional):**
  - Zone 1 (within 5 km): $850 per semester ($1,700 per academic year)
  - Zone 2 (5 km to 15 km): $1,200 per semester ($2,400 per academic year)
  - Zone 3 (over 15 km): $1,500 per semester ($3,000 per academic year)
- **Cafeteria Meal Plan (Optional):**
  - Full Lunch Plan: $650 per semester
  - Breakfast and Lunch Combo Plan: $900 per semester
  - Individual meals may also be purchased daily via the student campus card.
- **Advanced Examination and AP/IB Fees:** $125 per exam registration.

## Sibling Discounts and Financial Assistance

- **Sibling Discount:**
  - Second enrolled child: 10% discount on annual tuition.
  - Third and subsequent enrolled children: 15% discount on annual tuition.
  - Sibling discounts apply to the younger child's tuition rate.

- **Financial Aid and Merit Scholarships:**
  - Greenfield International School offers need-based tuition assistance covering up to 40% of tuition costs.
  - Merit scholarships are available for high-achieving applicants entering Grades 9 through 11.
  - Financial aid applications must be submitted to the Admissions and Financial Aid Committee by 15 May for the following academic year.

## Withdrawal and Refund Policy

If a student withdraws from Greenfield International School, refunds on tuition are processed according to the following schedule:

- **Written withdrawal notice received before the first day of the semester:** 100% tuition refund (excluding the non-refundable registration/deposit fee).
- **Withdrawal within the first 10 school days of the semester:** 80% tuition refund.
- **Withdrawal between school days 11 and 20 of the semester:** 50% tuition refund.
- **Withdrawal after 20 school days of the semester:** No refund is granted.

Optional service fees (such as transportation and cafeteria meal plans) are refunded on a prorated monthly basis upon student withdrawal.
