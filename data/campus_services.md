# Campus Services and Student Procedures

**School:** Greenfield International School
**Document type:** Fictional campus services information for RAG testing
**Last updated:** 1 September 2026

## Reception office

The reception office is open Monday through Friday from 7:45 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Students who arrive after 8:00 a.m. must check in at reception and receive a late pass. Visitors should report to reception when they arrive on campus.

For general questions, contact office@greenfield.example.edu or call +1 555 010 2040.

## Library and learning center

The library is open Monday through Friday from 7:45 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Students may borrow up to five books for three weeks. A renewal is allowed when another student has not reserved the item. Books should be returned or renewed before the due date.

Students can ask library staff for help locating sources, using the learning center, or planning independent study. Students must follow quiet-study expectations and handle library materials carefully.

## Cafeteria

Breakfast is served from 7:30 a.m. to 7:55 a.m. Lunch is served from 11:45 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. Vegetarian and common allergy-aware options are available.

Families should record food allergies and dietary restrictions in the student health profile. Students with a food-related concern should speak to cafeteria staff, the school nurse, or a trusted adult.

## School transportation

Optional bus routes are available for registered families. Morning bus service begins at 7:00 a.m. Afternoon routes leave campus at 3:30 p.m. Students must remain seated, wear a seat belt, and follow the driver's instructions.

Transportation questions should be sent to transport@greenfield.example.edu. Families should notify the transportation office when a registered student will not use an assigned route.

## Health and wellbeing

The school provides a school nurse, counselor, learning support, language support, and safeguarding officer. Students can request an appointment through a teacher, reception, or studentservices@greenfield.example.edu.

Medication brought to school must be registered with the nurse unless a documented self-carry plan has been approved. Students should report an injury, illness, safety concern, abuse, or threat immediately to a trusted adult.

For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services first. Notify the school reception office when it is safe to do so.

## Technology support

The IT help desk supports school accounts, password concerns, and suspected account compromise. Students must protect their passwords and use school accounts for educational purposes only.

Suspected account compromise or unsafe online behavior should be reported immediately to helpdesk@greenfield.example.edu or to a teacher. Students must not access harmful, illegal, or age-inappropriate material using school networks or devices.

## Clubs and room bookings

Students who want to start a club should submit a short proposal, identify a staff sponsor, and collect interest from at least eight students. Approved clubs may use classrooms after school until 4:15 p.m. on weekdays, subject to room availability.

Club schedules are published by student services at the beginning of each semester. Questions about clubs should be sent to studentservices@greenfield.example.edu.
