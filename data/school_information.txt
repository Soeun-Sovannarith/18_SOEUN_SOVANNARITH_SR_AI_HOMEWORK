ABOUT THE SCHOOL
Greenfield International School is a fictional co-educational day school serving students from Grade 7 through Grade 12. The school combines an English-language academic program with community projects, creative arts, athletics, and student well-being support.

School address: 24 Greenfield Avenue, Riverside District
Main phone: +1 555 010 2040
General email: office@greenfield.example.edu
Website: https://greenfield.example.edu

SCHOOL HOURS
The campus is open from 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. on regular school days.
Classes run from 8:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m., Monday through Friday.
The reception office is open from 7:45 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
The school is closed on weekends and public holidays unless a scheduled event is announced.

ACADEMIC PROGRAM
All students take English, mathematics, science, social studies, physical education, digital literacy, and a second language. Students in Grades 10-12 choose electives such as computer science, visual arts, economics, environmental studies, music, and design technology.

The academic year has two semesters:
- Semester 1: September through January
- Semester 2: February through June

Progress reports are shared with families at the end of each semester. Families can contact the relevant subject teacher to discuss academic progress.

ADMISSIONS
Families begin an application by submitting the online application form, previous school records, proof of identity, and emergency contact information. Applicants may be invited to a student interview and age-appropriate placement assessment. Admission decisions are sent by email.

The admissions office can be reached at admissions@greenfield.example.edu. Applications are normally reviewed within ten school days after all required documents are received.

STUDENT SUPPORT
The school provides a counselor, a school nurse, learning support, language support, and a safeguarding officer. Students can request an appointment through a teacher, the reception office, or studentservices@greenfield.example.edu. Urgent safety concerns should be reported immediately to any trusted adult on campus.

LIBRARY AND LEARNING CENTER
The library is open Monday through Friday from 7:45 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Students may borrow up to five books for three weeks. A renewal is allowed when another student has not reserved the item. Students must return or renew materials before the due date.

TRANSPORTATION
The school offers optional bus routes for registered families. Bus service begins at 7:00 a.m. and afternoon routes leave campus at 3:30 p.m. Students must remain seated, wear a seat belt, and follow the driver's instructions. Transportation questions should be sent to transport@greenfield.example.edu.

MEALS
The cafeteria serves breakfast from 7:30 a.m. to 7:55 a.m. and lunch from 11:45 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. Vegetarian and common allergy-aware options are available. Families should record food allergies and dietary restrictions in the student health profile.
