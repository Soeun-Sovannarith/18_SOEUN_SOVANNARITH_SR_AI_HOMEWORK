# Academic Calendar and Learning Guide

**School:** Greenfield International School
**Document type:** Fictional academic information for RAG testing
**Academic year:** September 2026 to June 2027

## Academic year

The school year is divided into two semesters. Semester 1 runs from September through January, and Semester 2 runs from February through June. The school publishes the detailed calendar before the start of each semester.

Progress reports are shared with families at the end of each semester. Families who have questions about a progress report should contact the relevant subject teacher first. The grade coordinator can help when a question involves several subjects or broader academic planning.

## Weekly learning schedule

Regular classes run Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m. Students should arrive by 7:45 a.m. and report to homeroom by 7:55 a.m. The daily timetable may include lessons, advisory time, laboratory work, physical education, and supervised study.

Students should bring the materials listed by each teacher. Laboratory sessions, workshops, and physical education may require special clothing or equipment. Teachers announce major projects and assessment dates in advance when possible.

## Assessment and coursework

Students are expected to submit their own work and acknowledge sources used in assignments. A student who needs help understanding an assignment should ask the subject teacher before the due date. Teachers may provide clarification, examples, or an approved extension when circumstances justify it.

During tests and examinations, students must follow the invigilator's instructions. Possible academic misconduct is reviewed by the academic integrity committee. Possible responses include a retake, mark penalty, reflection task, or another proportionate response.

## Academic support

Students may request learning support or language support through a teacher, the reception office, or studentservices@greenfield.example.edu. The school counselor can help students plan study routines, manage school-related concerns, and identify additional support.

Students who are absent should ask their subject teachers about missed work after returning to school. Families should notify the attendance office before 9:00 a.m. when a student is absent.
