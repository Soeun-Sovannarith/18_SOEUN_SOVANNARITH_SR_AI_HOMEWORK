# Greenfield International School

## Student Regulations and Code of Conduct

**Document type:** Fictional school policy for RAG testing  
**Effective date:** 1 September 2026  
**Review cycle:** Annually  
**Applies to:** Students, visitors, and school-sponsored activities

> This is sample content created for a school RAG application. It is not an official regulation of a real school.

## 1. Purpose

These regulations create a safe, respectful, inclusive, and productive learning environment. Students are expected to follow the rules on campus, during online learning, on school transportation, and at school-sponsored events.

## 2. Attendance and punctuality

- Students should arrive at school by 7:45 a.m. and report to homeroom by 7:55 a.m.
- The school day runs from 8:00 a.m. to 3:15 p.m., Monday through Friday.
- Parents or guardians must notify the attendance office before 9:00 a.m. when a student is absent.
- Students who arrive after 8:00 a.m. must check in at the reception office and receive a late pass.
- Planned leave should be requested at least three school days in advance, except in emergencies.
- Repeated unexplained absence or lateness may result in a meeting with the student, family, and grade coordinator.

## 3. Respectful behavior

Students must treat classmates, staff, visitors, and school property with care. The following behaviors are prohibited:

- Bullying, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats.
- Physical aggression, fighting, or deliberately damaging property.
- Hate speech, humiliating language, or sharing private images without permission.
- Cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or falsifying school records.
- Disrupting lessons, assemblies, examinations, or safety procedures.

Concerns should be reported to a teacher, counselor, grade coordinator, or the safeguarding officer. Reports are handled discreetly, and retaliation for making a good-faith report is prohibited.

## 4. Uniform and personal appearance

- Students must wear the approved school uniform on regular school days.
- Closed-toe shoes are required for laboratories, workshops, and physical education.
- Clothing and accessories must be clean, safe, and suitable for a learning environment.
- Religious, cultural, and medically necessary accommodations can be requested through the student services office.

## 5. Technology and acceptable use

- Personal devices must be silenced and kept away during lessons unless a teacher authorizes their use.
- Students may use school accounts only for educational purposes and must protect their passwords.
- Recording, photographing, or livestreaming another person requires that person's permission and must follow school privacy rules.
- Students must not access harmful, illegal, or age-inappropriate material using school networks or devices.
- Suspected account compromise or unsafe online behavior must be reported immediately to the IT help desk or a teacher.

## 6. Health, safety, and safeguarding

- Students must follow staff instructions during drills, evacuations, laboratory activities, and trips.
- Weapons, fireworks, dangerous items, and unauthorized medication are not permitted.
- Alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, and illegal drugs are prohibited on school property and at school events.
- Students should tell an adult immediately about a safety concern, injury, abuse, or threat.
- Medication brought to school must be registered with the nurse unless a documented self-carry plan has been approved.

## 7. Assessment integrity

Students are responsible for submitting their own work and acknowledging sources. During tests and examinations, students must follow the invigilator's instructions. Possible academic misconduct is reviewed by the academic integrity committee, which may assign a retake, mark penalty, reflection task, or other proportionate response.

## 8. Consequences and restorative response

The school considers the seriousness of the behavior, the student's age, intent, prior record, and impact on others. Responses may include a restorative conversation, warning, family meeting, loss of privilege, supervised detention, behavior agreement, suspension, or referral to the appropriate authority when required by law.

Students will normally have an opportunity to explain what happened and participate in a fair review. Emergency safety actions may be taken immediately when necessary.

## 9. Questions and appeals

Questions about these regulations should be sent to the grade coordinator or student services office. A family may request a review of a significant disciplinary decision in writing within five school days of receiving the decision.
